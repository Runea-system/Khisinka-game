KISHINKAI: Shadow Path is a non-commercial fan project, made as a technical demo of browser-based 3D ninja combat.

All world names, characters, techniques, clan emblems, and visual assets in this game are original creations and are not affiliated with any anime or manga franchise.

  • Creator game by @Kishinka_play
  • Design & Code — Independent Studio
  • 3D Engine — Three.js
  • Typography — Yuji Syuku, Zen Kaku Gothic New, JetBrains Mono (Google Fonts)
  • Audio — procedurally synthesized in-browser

Made for learning & entertainment purposes. Any resemblance to other works is unintentional.
